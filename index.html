      Materials

      Manage

      A map holds up to fifteen materials: its ground, its water, the world’s other surfaces, its rock types’ materials and its own for landforms and the Paint brush. They are saved with the map and with a world you save.

      Drag from an output dot to an input to connect. Right-click or Tab adds a node. Double-click a node to view it alone. Delete removes.

      Moves all of the material’s color steps together, and its palette with it; each step stays editable in its Gradient node.

      This material publishes no controls yet. Select a node and press ◆ beside a setting to list it here, so the material can be adjusted without opening its graph.

      Select a node to edit it. A node’s picture shows what it makes; the Material node on the right takes the color, height and shine.

      A frame only labels its nodes. Removing it leaves them in place.

      The game draws in 256 colors. Choose the ones this material may use; it is reduced to them by error diffusion after its graph makes the color. Click a color to put it in or out.

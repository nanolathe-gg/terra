Rock types › Colors & recipe

The rock’s color is what its faces wear, lit and weathered by the settings below; the bed slots color its Rock column’s beds where Strata shows them. Absorption is the color thick translucent rock deepens toward and its cast shadow takes (Rock translucency); unless one is chosen, it is the rock’s own color. The square after each color is the palette entry the game draws it with (before dithering).

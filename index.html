    Material recipe

    Positive relief raises the surface; negative relief cuts pits. Surface amount blends gloss, roughness, and wetness through the pattern.

    Mask

    Build the base pattern, then layer fine grain and marks. Noise octaves add broader, stronger layers; secondary detail adds a finer layer.

    Marks

    Displace the noise with another periodic noise field. Axis balance at −1 displaces X only, 0 displaces both axes, and +1 displaces Y only.

    Phase & broad variation

    Remap noise values before they drive color and bump: adjust the range and power, invert, hard-clip, or shape them into sine bands.

    Value filters

    Map the pattern into three colors. Fine-grain tints and mark blending live with their noise components. Color softness blends linear and smooth interpolation.

Verdict per kind, round 4 (compared directly with retail)

The clean room is over: every kind below was iterated against retail sheets at 1x and 3x on my own machine (the sheets are never published and no retail pixel is in the generators or on this page). Short answer: fire, deposits and rocks moved the most and now read as the same kind of object as retail; none is a copy. Differences marked "by eye" are what I still see beside retail.

  • Fire. A smooth heat-field flame replaced per-pixel noise: contiguous 2-6 px patches, pale core, yellow body, red only as a rim and at tongue tips, hooked tongue tips, rounded lead-in lumps centered on the trunk, a gradual burn-out and a quick crown collapse that shows black limbs. Measured: index 208 share 0.215 (retail 0.221), 195 0.129 (0.131), overlap of consecutive frames 0.56 (0.57). By eye retail's flame is more ragged and thinner, with sharper notches, and the broadleaf crown is bigger than mine; the shrub flame is smoother and taller than retail's speckled one.
  • Burnt broadleaf. A thick gnarled trunk with few kinked limbs that splay sideways and 1 px twigs; 580 px (retail 493-607). By eye retail's limbs are thinner and more numerous, and it has teal-blue highlights I do not copy.
  • Burnt conifer. Lopsided, ragged, lit from the left with lighter grays, longer drooping twigs. By eye retail has more pinkish-gray light pixels.
  • Deposits. Grainy stone (dark-to-light lilac/steel/white ramp, per-pixel jitter), ragged slabs, a tight speckled moss rim of 3-6 px. By eye close at 1x; retail's small plates sit a little closer and its stone is a bit whiter.
  • Rocks. A tilted oval with fine pink grain, a dark rim all round and a large offset shadow. By eye retail's outline is lumpier and its pink is finer still.
  • Shrubs. Brighter yellow-green tufts (163/164/165) with thin dark undersides; retail is still brighter and its tufts fuller.
  • Trees. More contrast, bright yellow-green flecks on the lit lobes, lighter gray-olive speckle on conifers; no pale non-green pixels. Retail silhouettes are hand-drawn and more varied.
  • Scars and vents: unchanged. Not built: tree death and reclaim animations.

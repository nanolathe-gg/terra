Round 5 followed the reviewer's read of round 4. Retail comparison sheets stay on my machine; this page has generated images only. "By eye" means beside retail.

  • Fire. Torn edges, holes, 6-10 detached licks and 2-4 thin wisps per frame, tongues climbing the crown flanks, the flame swaying off the trunk, a narrower ragged foot; heat-field colors kept. Tail fire share over frames 91-95 now 0.77 / 0.66 / 0.59 / 0.43 / 0.15 (retail 0.77 / 0.74 / 0.61 / 0.34 / 0.16). Overlap of consecutive frames 0.51 (retail 0.57). By eye retail still has more floating fragments and finer wisps, and its flame body is thinner.
  • Rocks. Lumpier angular outline with a tilt, broad tonal planes, whiter highlights, pink only as soft areas, no olive-yellow specks. Rim -48 (retail -43 to -71). By eye retail's pink is more widespread and subtle; the base of mine is still darker than retail's (bottom-row delta -94 against -46 to -58).
  • Deposits. Plates at least 3 px apart with moss between them, crisper edges, brighter lilac-white stone over steel-blue. By eye the RockMetal3 cluster is still more compact than retail's ragged mass.
  • Burnt broadleaf. Thinner, more numerous limbs, trunk narrowing upward (12 px at the base to about 7 at the fork). By eye retail's trunk is heavier at mid height.
  • Shrubs. A little brighter. Retail tufts are still fuller.
  • Trees, conifers, burnt conifers, vents and scars were left alone. Not built: tree death and reclaim.

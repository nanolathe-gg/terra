Verdict per kind (honest, from the owner's question: is this possible?)

Short answer: yes for plausible, no for retail-equal yet. Statistics land inside the spec for most scalars (69 of 98 per-kind metric means, measured, table at the bottom), but I have never seen retail, so every "looks like" below is by eye against the spec's words only. The owner's side-by-side is the real test.

  • Broadleaf trees: plausible. Size, fill, edge raggedness, stray pixels, light gradient, dark base and the 31/20/17 percent color bands are close (measured). Weak spots: all crowns read as the same rounded egg (silhouette variety is low), color count is 40 against 51 to 59, and the leaf mottling matches statistics, not necessarily the eye.
  • Conifers: plausible. Teardrop, tiers, brown branch pixels in the lower half. Too few distinct colors (41 against 44 to 47) and a smoother edge (5.7 against 6.7 to 7.1).
  • Burnt trees: plausible. Forked skeletons with twigs, ash and a pale base. Slightly too few pixels (445 against 493 to 627) and the widest part sits too high (by the numbers).
  • Shrubs: plausible, thin. Right palette, no shadow, dark undersides, but about half the retail pixel count and the dark rim is weaker than retail's (measured).
  • Rocks: plausible, not retail quality. Rim, base darkening and grain are present; facets are crude and there is little of the cream and pink highlight structure the spec describes (by eye). Same-color clusters are larger than retail's.
  • Scars (smudges, stains): plausible. Dark stipple cores, lighter edges, stipple shadow plus pebbles; smudge edges are a little too ragged (measured 8.8 against 5.5 to 7.3).
  • Metal deposits: plausible. Separate pale chunks in a mossy halo painted into whatever ground is under them; chunk count tracks metal. Distinct colors are far below retail's 84 to 91 (about 40, measured), and the chunks have little facet detail.
  • Vents: plausible but plain. Black forking cracks with a thin moss and brown edge; size inside the spec. Not as rich as the spec's 57-color description.
  • Burning: works, not there yet. 96 frames, charring crown, skeleton at the end, flame flicker (frame change 0.78 against 0.74 to 0.77, fire share 0.48 against 0.36 to 0.49, measured). Mean flame height about 63 px against 77 (measured). Tree death and reclaim animations are not built.

Everything above is deterministic per seed. Retail-equal quality would need the owner's eye on a pair sheet, then another tuning pass per kind.

Verdict per kind, round 3 (honest)

Short answer: plausible across the board; fire, rocks, shrubs, burnt trees and deposits now follow the auditor's retail numbers closely, and the page's table shows where they still miss. I have still not seen retail, so "by eye" is against the spec's words only. The Round 2 versus Round 3 rows show the same seeds before and after.

  • Fire: rebuilt on retail's structure, close in structure, not yet in look. 9-frame lead-in under the green crown, a one-cut switch to charred gray at frame 9, a 14-frame cycle that repeats byte for byte (39 unique frames of 96, retail 36-43), a 16-frame tail ending on the burnt tree's exact size and anchor with 15% embers. The base is an arch (2.8 px below the anchor near the trunk, retail 2-4). By eye the flame is still one tall, solid column and the tongues are less distinct than retail's; sparks, color layers and overlap (0.47 against 0.57) are close.
  • Burnt broadleaf: close in kind. A heavy gnarled trunk, flared base, forks into limbs and twigs, grays and near-black, browns at the base. Pixel count is high (about 680 against 493-607) and the twig crown is busier than retail's by eye.
  • Burnt conifer: closer. Sparse, see-through, thin trunk, isolated pixels 0.21 (top of the range), gray 0.20 (just under 0.22-0.26).
  • Rocks: much closer. One smooth, lumpy, tilted oblong; light falls from upper left to a dark lower right; cream, pink and white are soft mottling plus one broad white highlight; dark edge only on the shadow side. Band shares hold (highlight 0.38 against 0.383). By eye the surface may be slightly too pitted.
  • Shrubs: plausible, no ring. Scatter of small yellow-green clumps with dark undersides. Dense: fill 0.36, largest blob 0.77. Sparse: 24 blobs, largest 0.20. Bright 162/163 greens are still on the low side of retail's range.
  • Metal deposits: retail look. Plates in lilac-white, pink-lilac and steel blue/gray; two looks by metal (separate plates, ragged cluster with thin arms); one merged moss patch. Stone pixels track metal (207, 691, 847, 795 against 198, 564, 806, 886). Stone is still 0.20 of the painted pixels against 0.09-0.18. The round pebbles of round 1 remain as an artist style.

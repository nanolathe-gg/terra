Verdict per kind, round 8 (compared directly with retail)

Round 8 is the fire work plus the owner's six notes. Retail sheets and animation strips stay on my machine (the owner has them in the compare folder).

  • Fire. A slimmer, taller column with blunt tongue tips, hard-edged color patches with pixel-level jitter instead of smooth gradients, 4-7 curling 2 px licks per frame, more crown showing, detached bits beside the body. Measured against retail Tree1 (frames 9-22): width per row at 3 / 8 / 16 / 30 rows below the top retail 6.0 / 5.5 / 7.6 / 11.1, ours 2.2 / 4.5 / 7.4 / 17.5 (the lower body is still too wide); crown share of drawn pixels retail 0.48, ours 0.42. Edge per fire pixel: retail 0.355, ours 0.27. Tail over frames 91-95, retail 0.77 / 0.74 / 0.61 / 0.34 / 0.16: broadleaf 0.77 / 0.76 / 0.62 / 0.42 / 0.15, conifer 0.80 / 0.77 / 0.58 / 0.39 / 0.15. Overlap 0.5 (retail 0.57). The foot was not narrowed: retail Tree1 has about 21 columns reaching the ground and ours 14. Embers stay on the burn-out frames; the burnt successor sprites carry none (tested).
  • Trees. Broadleaf crowns taper toward the top with irregular peaks; both are controls (taper, peaks, retail Green 0.5) and vary by seed. Bright leaf pixels are placed where the crown faces the sun and the sparkle follows the light.
  • Rocks. New shade control (default 0.6). The lower right never carries a highlight pixel (tested) and shades more strongly.
  • Deposits. Brushed horizontal streaks, a noisier grain and specular glints; no grass or moss is ever enclosed by a plate (tested). By eye retail's glints are denser and its moss rim darker.
  • Burnt trees, shrubs, conifers (other than the fire), vents and scars were left alone. Not built: tree death and reclaim.

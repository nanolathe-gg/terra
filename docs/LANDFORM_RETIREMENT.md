# Retiring the specialized terrain tools

**Retired, November 3.** Plateau, Hill (Rounded ridge), Mountain (Peaked ridge), Ramp, Deep water, Shallow water, Raise / lower and the hidden Lower kind are gone from the editor, the GPU path and the shaders. Their palette buttons stay and draw landforms: each starts from a numbers-only preset (`LANDFORM_TOOLS` in sculpt.js), and the Tool preset menu overlays each world's numbers per button (Moon's ridges lobed, Desert's ragged at the foot, Lava's water with lobes and shoreline steps, Ice's with shore plates). The separate Landform button merged into Plateau. Shore plates and Ice's angular facets moved to Landform. Crater and Crystals are unchanged; a crater's spurs keep the hidden ridge stroke (`spurs`) with Moon's lobes and Desert's ragged foot.

Every fixture was re-authored with landform strokes at the same index, from ground heights measured on main under each stroke; the reviewed retail-window water fits are now the maintained scenes' water, and the dedicated comparison halves are deleted. As the owner accepted, a ramp no longer re-joins its ends after earlier edits, and a Raise to landform does not add to the ground under it the way Hill and Raise / lower did. The rest of this page is the assessment that preceded the retirement.

**Retired, November 6.** Crater and Crystals are gone from the editor, the GPU path and the shaders too: the `crater` and `crystal` stroke kinds, the crater's hidden ridge stroke (`spurs`), their passes (`stampCrater`, `stampCrystal`, `stampColumns`, `stampBerg`, the point read), their 22 options (craterlets, ejecta, rim height, sectors and gaps, crest jag and variation, crystal form, size, count and lean, the smooth hill share, the berg's face, tilt and level top, Bowl facets and the crystal craters' facet width, step and skin), the spurs' outline knobs (`moonLobes`, `baseVary`, `baseBump`), their world values, panel groups, audits and tests. Their palette buttons stay and draw landforms from numbers-only presets (`LANDFORM_TOOLS` crater and crystal; each world's numbers in `TOOL_PRESETS`), and a landform's palette entry is the button whose preset draws its section form (lfCrater, lfCrystal), whatever else it carries. What the two tools did is now generic landform features, gated by what a stroke's numbers do, never by its kind or world: a signed section (knots below 0) and the Ground height reference, so a crater is a bowl and rim dug into the ground it lands on, on a slope too; Ejecta and Secondaries, which make a drag aim the impact; spurs off a signed section's peak; and one Facets group (on and coverage, Facet shape: angular facets, stepped columns or prisms, Facet width, Step height, Surface Rock or Crystal, and each shape's own rows). Angular facets on a landform that digs are the old Bowl facets; prisms scatter over the shape without rows, never merge, and below Level tops 100 take a flat top tilted with the section (Ice's Crystals button draws such an iceberg). The 47 strokes of the ten windows that used the two tools (moon-1..3, red-1, crystal-1..3, ice-1..3) were re-authored as landforms at the same index. As the owner decided, a landform that digs below the sea makes water (the crater's dry floor went), Face roughness only roughens (Surface chooses the material), and the crater's crest variation went with its spurs. An old save's crater or crystal stroke loads without it, with one message saying how many were left out. The prototypes behind this are `prototype/lfcrater` and `prototype/lfcrystal`, the landing `lfland`.
